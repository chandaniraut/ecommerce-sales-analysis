# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Sample Superstore** dataset using **Python** to uncover valuable business insights. The analysis focuses on sales trends, profitability, product performance, and customer segments to help understand business performance and support better decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The project answers the following business questions:

- Calculate monthly sales.
- Identify the highest and lowest sales month.
- Analyze sales by product category.
- Find the highest and lowest selling category.
- Analyze sales by sub-category.
- Analyze monthly profit.
- Identify the month with the highest profit.
- Analyze profit by category.
- Analyze profit by sub-category.
- Analyze sales and profit by customer segment.
- Calculate the Sales-to-Profit Ratio.
